Questions people ask us.

What does Sarvenex do?

We build websites, mobile apps, custom software and WhatsApp automation for businesses, and we make our own ready-made products such as ChamberBook (for clinics and pharmacies) and 1 Local Stop (for shops).

Where are you based? Do you work outside West Bengal?

We are based in Nadia, West Bengal. Most work happens online, so we work with clients anywhere in India.

How much does a website or app cost?

It depends on the pages, features and integrations you need. After a short call we send a written scope and a fixed quote, so you know the cost before work starts.

Can my website or app be in Bengali?

Yes. We build in English, Bengali and Hindi, and can add a language switch so your customers choose.

Do you provide hosting and maintenance?

Yes. We can handle domain, hosting, SSL, email and backups, and offer monthly care plans for updates and fixes.

How do I get a demo of ChamberBook or 1 Local Stop?

Message us on WhatsApp and we will show you a live demo and help you set it up.

Have something worth building?

Tell us what you are working on. We will reply with a practical next step, usually within one working day.
